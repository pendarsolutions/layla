Layla reads Persian text and answers your questions about it.

For each question it picks one of the options you gave it, and says how sure it is.

Try itCreate a key

You send a text

A support message, a shopper's review, a news headline or a text message. Layla reads Persian.

You ask your questions

Each question brings its own options: one of several, yes or no, or a level. Layla answers best when they're asked in Persian; here they're shown in English.

  1. One of several

    Which team is this message for?

    • Sales
    • Support
    • Billing

  2. Yes or no

    Does the customer want to cancel the order?

    • Yes
    • No

  3. A level

    How urgent is this message?

    • Normal
    • Important
    • Urgent

Layla never writes text of its own. Every answer is one of the options you gave it, with its probability.

Layla picks one and says how sure it is

When the options are close, the answer is marked unsure: pass that message to a person.

Answers arrive one by one

Layla reads the text once for each question, and each answer arrives the moment it's ready. On a standard laptop GPU, each answer takes under 50 milliseconds.

  1. 0 ms{"event": "start", "outputs": ["team", "cancel", "urgency"]}
  2. 31 ms{"event": "result", "result": {"id": "team", "probability": 0.85, …}}
  3. 63 ms{"event": "result", "result": {"id": "cancel", "probability": 0.86, …}}
  4. 94 ms{"event": "result", "result": {"id": "urgency", "probability": 0.75, …}}
  5. 95 ms{"event": "done", "usage": {"input_tokens": 51}}
